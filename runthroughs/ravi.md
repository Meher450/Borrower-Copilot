## How the key domain challenges were handled

- **Declared income understating true cash flow (§6.3):** the ₹35,000 documented (ITR-derived) figure was blended with the ₹60,000 cash-income estimate (40%/60% weighting favouring the cash estimate, per `RULES.md`) rather than capping Ravi at the lower figure. Effective income used: ₹50,000/month.
- **No bureau history + unencumbered collateral (§6.3):** collateral (₹45L) exceeds the amount requested, so Ravi is routed to secured-product reasoning - his fair rate band (8.06–12.44%) is materially lower than the unsecured self-employed default (10–22%, seen in the must-only run below), and the Card explains why.
- **Divergence between sanction and safe-carry:** the two figures differ (₹13.07–16.81L sanction vs ₹8.24–10.59L safe-carry) - the app states the safe-carry figure should be relied on.
